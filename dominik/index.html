Dominik Samociuk

Information security manager at Future Processing

Dominik started as an independent security researcher and went through a variety of roles in his professional career. Combining his professional work with the constant search for new knowledge, he received a PhD in computer science. He is an author or co-author of more than 20 scientific and popular science articles and a holder of various certifications, e.g. CISSP, OSCP. He is constantly finding new challenges.
